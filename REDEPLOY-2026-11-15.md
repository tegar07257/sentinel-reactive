## Pair #2 (first hardened attempt) — FAILED on callback gas, superseded

Deployed ~11:2x WIB with callbackGasLimit **200,000**:
Sentinel `0xcc0C0837DABbbfCf4138073Aa46fEaf092C0a031` (Lasna),
callback `0x649Da92eA138F5Dc6A787c4E8D0ABFcE501D774d` (Base Sepolia).
Detections fired correctly (6 sells at the 0.05 threshold), but **all 6
callback deliveries failed**: the proxy emitted
`CallbackFailure(address,bytes)` (topic
`0xc8313f695443128e273f1edfcec40b94b7deea8dfbeafd0043290d6601d999db`)
and `lastAlertCount` stayed 0.

Root cause (proven, not guessed):
- The Base Sepolia callback proxy
  (`0x476F44b11F5C942c58f4c8E3d0300E5710743585`) withholds
  `_extraGas = 80,000` + `_maxChargeGas = 50,000` from the requested
  callback gas limit before forwarding (per
  https://dev.reactive.network/economy — "Not All Requested Gas
  Reaches the Target"). 200,000 − 130,000 = **70,000 forwarded**.
- `SentinelCallback.alert()` needs **~91,786 gas** (eth_estimateGas;
  eth_call with a 70,000 / 85,000 cap runs out of gas, 100,000 works):
  three cold SSTOREs + the Alert event.
- 70,000 < 91,786 → guaranteed out-of-gas → CallbackFailure, charged
  in full. Pair #1's 1,000,000 limit forwarded ~870,000 and worked.
- Fix: 500,000 forwards ~370,000 (≈4× the requirement) while still
  capping callback gas at half of pair #1's limit. Pair #3 verified
  live: 6/6 deliveries landed.
- `callbackGasLimit` is `immutable` in Sentinel.sol — no setter, so
  the fix required a full redeploy of the pair (the callback's
  `_CALLBACK_SENDER` is also immutable).

## Operating cost — the honest caveat (applies to pair #3 too)

The full loop works, but Lasna-side billing for pairs #2/#3 measured
**≈0.06–0.077 lREACT per detection cycle** (pair #3: 0.55 lREACT of
funding consumed over construction + 6 cycles, ending reserves 0 /
debt 0.011330 before the revival top-up). Pair #1 measured
≈0.0199/cycle on 2026-11-15's top-up burst, which matches the
documented fee formula `fee = p_base × C × (g_used + _extraGas)`
(100 gwei × 1.0 × (~94k + 100k) ≈ 0.0194) using receipt gas. The
excess for the new pairs is **not explained** by basefee (flat
100 gwei), by receipt gasUsed (91k–155k, same range as pair #1), or
by the callback gas limit (200k and 500k pairs cost about the same).
It is recorded here as an open measurement discrepancy, not papered
over. Practical consequence, same advice as the checklist: **top up
~0.5 lREACT via `depositTo` immediately before any demo/submission
session and demo right away** — at the observed burn rate that buys
roughly 6–8 detection cycles, and organic bursts can drain it in
minutes. The contract debt-pauses when reserves run out (debt > 0);
that is the network's by-design behaviour, not the old trigger bug —
detections and callbacks themselves are proven working.
